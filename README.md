## 👤 About Me

I'm a **Data Science Engineering student** at LEAD University (Costa Rica) and **Junior Data Architect at WeCode**, focused on building scalable data pipelines, AI-powered extraction systems, and business intelligence solutions.

My work sits at the intersection of **data engineering**, **applied AI**, and **cloud infrastructure** — transforming unstructured information into structured, actionable assets. I design lean, production-ready pipelines using Python, SQL, and Generative AI APIs, with a strong emphasis on correctness, efficiency, and minimal redundancy.

I approach every problem with an **engineering-first mindset**: no over-engineering, no unnecessary abstractions — just clean, purposeful systems that solve real problems at scale.
